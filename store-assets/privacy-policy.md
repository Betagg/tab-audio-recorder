# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-25

Dolphin Tab Audio Recorder is a Chrome extension that records audio from the active browser tab and saves it locally as an MP3 file.

## Data Processing

Dolphin Tab Audio Recorder runs locally in Chrome. It does not upload recordings or metadata to any server.

The extension may process the following information locally:

- Audio from the active browser tab after the user starts recording.
- Page title, source URL, website host, recording duration, save time, and filename for local recording history.

## Data Storage

Recordings are encoded locally and downloaded through Chrome's download manager.

Recording history is stored locally in the browser's extension storage. History entries contain lightweight metadata only and do not contain audio files.

Dolphin keeps up to 50 local history entries. Users can open Recording history and choose Clear history to remove those entries. Clearing history does not delete MP3 files that Chrome already downloaded.

## Data Sharing

Dolphin Tab Audio Recorder does not sell, transfer, share, or upload user data.

## Analytics and Tracking

Dolphin Tab Audio Recorder does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or behavioral tracking.

## Accounts

Dolphin Tab Audio Recorder does not require an account.

## Microphone

Dolphin Tab Audio Recorder does not capture microphone audio. It captures audio from the active browser tab only after the user starts recording.

## Permissions

- `tabCapture`: captures audio from the active Chrome tab after the user starts recording.
- `activeTab` and `scripting`: read lightweight page title and URL metadata after user action for filename suggestions and local history.
- `offscreen`: keeps local recording and MP3 encoding running while the popup is closed.
- `downloads`: saves the generated MP3 through Chrome's download manager.

## Website Services and Protected Media

Websites may use their own cookies, analytics, accounts, and media protections. Those services are separate from Dolphin Tab Audio Recorder.

Some protected or DRM-based media may not be available to Chrome tab capture. Dolphin does not bypass access controls.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the support contact provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
